💡Football jerseys are cut wide to fit over pads. For everyday wear, most people go with their usual size.

📏 Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, under your arms. This is the best way to pick a size.

📐 Or measure a shirt

Lay a jersey that fits you well flat, measure armpit to armpit, and double it.

🔒 Made to order

Jerseys can't be exchanged once production starts, so double-check before you order. We're happy to help.
