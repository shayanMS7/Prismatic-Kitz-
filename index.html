Our mission

Built by fans, for fans.

Prismatic Kitz started with a group of friends who love sports. We love repping our favourite teams, but we also know how expensive jerseys are, especially when new kits drop every single season.

We figured there had to be a better way. Our mission is to help people get jerseys for their favourite teams every year, without spending absurd amounts of money every season.

And if you're buying for kids who outgrow their jerseys every year, you shouldn't have to spend hundreds of dollars to keep them in their team's colours.
